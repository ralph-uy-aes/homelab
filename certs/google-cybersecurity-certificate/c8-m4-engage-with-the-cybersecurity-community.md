# Course 8 – Module 4: Engage with the Cybersecurity Community

---

## Strategies for Engaging with the Cybersecurity Community

### Security Organizations and Conferences
- Opportunity to gain knowledge from seasoned professionals
- Stay up-to-date on security strategies and techniques

### Finding the Right Organization
1. **Identify your interests** – Incident response, forensic security, data logging, cloud security, etc.
2. **Search online** – Use keywords like "incident response cybersecurity conferences in my area"
3. **Use social media** – LinkedIn® groups and organizations

### Sample LinkedIn® Search Queries
- "Incident response cybersecurity groups"
- "Organizations for cybersecurity analysts"

### Be Aware of Social Engineering
- Hackers use social media to trick users into giving up private information
- **Never click on unexpected links or attachments from unfamiliar users**

### Cybersecurity Mailing Lists (CISA)
- Security threat information and best practices
- Weekly summaries of new vulnerabilities

---

## Build Your Cybersecurity Network

### Connect with CISOs on LinkedIn®
- CISOs stay up-to-date on all security aspects
- Following them provides access to useful tips and relevant news
- Helps sharpen your security mindset

### Tips for Connecting on LinkedIn®
| Tip | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| Use a conversational tone | Sound natural and approachable |
| Provide a clear reason | Explain why you want to connect |
| Avoid errors | Check spelling and grammar |
| Don't appear suspicious | Never ask for downloads or unusual actions |

### Example Connection Message
> "Hi, Tim. I recently completed the Google Cybersecurity Certificate program, and I'd like to connect with other security professionals. It seems like you have a lot of experience in the security industry that I can learn from. Let's keep in touch!"
