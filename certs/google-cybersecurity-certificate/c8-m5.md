## Introduction to AI in Cybersecurity

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** – Computer programs designed to perform cognitive tasks typically associated with human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, and understanding language.

### AI in Cybersecurity
- Helps automate routine tasks
- Enhances productivity
- Strengthens defenses against cyber attacks

### The Double-Edged Sword
- AI is a powerful ally but also a target for malicious actors
- Cybercriminals can leverage AI to launch sophisticated attacks, evade detection, and exploit weaknesses

---

## Generative AI (Gen AI)

**Generative AI** – AI capable of creating new content (text, images, code, or other media) in response to prompts.

**Examples:** Gemini, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot

**Prompt** – Input provided to an AI tool to instruct it on what to generate.

### Applications in Cybersecurity

| Application | Example |
|-------------|---------|
| **Create content** | Generate fake data for testing security tools |
| **Analyze quickly** | Summarize reports or meeting transcripts |
| **Answer questions** | Research common cybersecurity threats |
| **Simplify work** | Initial analysis of suspicious emails |

---

## TCREI Framework for Effective Prompts

**TCREI** – Task, Context, References, Evaluate, Iterate

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Task** | Specific instruction or goal for the AI |
| **Context** | Necessary details to help AI understand your needs |
| **References** | Examples or specific information to guide AI output |
| **Evaluate** | Review output to determine if it meets expectations |
| **Iterate** | Refine and improve prompts through repeated cycles |

## Responsible Use of Generative AI

### Guidelines
1. **Review outputs carefully** – Check for accuracy and usefulness
2. **Disclose use** – Be transparent about AI usage
3. **Consider privacy/security** – Avoid entering sensitive information
4. **Human-in-the-loop** – AI should complement human skills, not replace them

**Human-in-the-loop approach** – Combines machine intelligence with human oversight, involvement, and validation to train, use, verify, and refine AI results.

## Technical Interview Preparation

### What Are Technical Interviews?
- Focus on required knowledge of specific tools
- May include coding questions (Python, pseudo code)
- Assess understanding of security concepts

### Key Technical Concepts to Review
- **Python** – Basic knowledge, automation applications
- **Security frameworks** – NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- **Network security** – TCP/IP model, OSI model
- **SIEM tools** – Purpose and applications

### Tips for Technical Interviews
1. **Write the question down** – Ensures you understand all parts
2. **Take your time** – Provide structured responses
3. **Use terminology confidently** – Demonstrates solid understanding

### Example Interview Questions
| Question | Answer Summary |
|----------|----------------|
| What is the TCP/IP model? | Framework for visualizing how data is organized and transmitted across a network (4 layers) |
| What is the OSI model? | 7‑layer standardized concept for computers to communicate and send data over a network |
| What are SIEM tools? | Security Information and Event Management tools used to identify and analyze threats, risks, and vulnerabilities |
