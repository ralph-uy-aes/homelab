## Data and Asset Classification

### Data Classification Types

| Classification | Description | Examples |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| **Public Data** | Already accessible to public; minimal risk if shared. | Press releases, job descriptions, marketing materials |
| **Private Data** | Should be kept from public; serious risk if exposed. | Company email addresses, employee IDs, research data |
| **Sensitive Data** | Must be protected; significant financial/reputational damage if exposed. | PII, SPII, PHI (bank accounts, SSNs, passwords, passport numbers, medical info) |
| **Confidential Data** | Limits on access; often requires NDAs. | Trade secrets, financial records, sensitive government data |

### Asset Classification Levels
- **Low-level assets** – Public data; minimal impact if compromised (e.g., company website address)
- **High-level assets** – Sensitive/confidential data; significant negative impact if leaked (e.g., internal emails with trade secrets)

### Information Lifecycle Strategy
1. **Identify** – Determine important assets (PII, financial info, SSNs, EINs)
2. **Assess** – Review security measures and policies (vulnerability scanning, processes)
3. **Protect** – Implement security controls; learn by observing senior analysts
4. **Monitor** – Continuously monitor implemented security processes

## Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

### Four-Part Security Process
1. **Identify** assets that must be protected
2. **Determine** potential threats to those assets
3. **Detect** potential threats (tools and processes)
4. **Create** business continuity and disaster recovery plans (IT or appropriate business function)

### Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
Document outlining procedures to sustain operations during and after significant disruption.

**Four Essential Steps:**
1. **Conduct a business impact analysis** – Assess possible effects of disrupted business functions
2. **Identify, document, and implement recovery steps** – Create actionable response steps
3. **Organize a business continuity team** – Members from cybersecurity, IT, HR, communications, operations
4. **Conduct training** – Practice different risk scenarios

### Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
Outlines steps to minimize impact of a security incident and resolve threats.

**Steps include:**
- Implement recovery strategies to restore software
- Implement recovery strategies to restore hardware functionality
- Identify impacted applications and data

## Security Mindset

**Security mindset** – The ability to evaluate risk and constantly seek out and identify potential or actual breaches of systems, applications, or data.
