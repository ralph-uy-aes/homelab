# IG.06 - Website Footprinting with Netcraft

### Practical Demo
- Netcraft
    - Used to gather info about a target domain
    - Gives you info for registrar, emails, OS, web stack, etc... for a host/domain
        - Basically gives you everything we did manually in the last video
    - Is just a website that you can pull site reports out of
    - Even gives you exploit analyses or potential exploits you could use
    - Gives you SPF and DKIM
    - Shows site technologies, server-side stack, client-side stack
    - Shows advertising networks, blog, etc...
- Overall, just a really good passive web recon swiss army knife
